About Stride

The job search is a marathon,
not a sprint.

We built Stride because we've been there — refreshing inboxes, juggling spreadsheets, losing track of which company you talked to last week. The modern job search is overwhelming. Dozens of applications. Countless follow-ups. The mental load of keeping it all straight while staying motivated.

Stride exists to bring order to the chaos.

We believe landing your next role isn't about luck — it's about organization, focus, and grit. It's about showing up every day, taking one step forward, and trusting the process. That's why we named it Stride. Not a sprint. Not a scramble. A steady, confident stride toward what's next.

One application. One follow-up. One conversation at a time.

Stride keeps everything in one place so you can focus on what actually matters: telling your story, building relationships, and finding the right fit. No more scattered notes. No more "wait, did I already apply there?" Just clarity, momentum, and progress you can see.

Your next chapter starts with a single step.
