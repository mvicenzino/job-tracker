About Stride

The job search is a marathon,
not a sprint.

That's why I built Stride — to help you take it one step at a time.

I've been there — refreshing inboxes, juggling spreadsheets, losing track of which company I talked to last week. The modern job search is overwhelming. Dozens of applications. Countless follow-ups. The mental load of keeping it all straight while staying motivated.

After going through the process myself, I realized there had to be a better way. Not another complicated tool with features you'll never use. Just something simple, focused, and actually helpful.

Stride exists to bring order to the chaos.

Why "Stride"?

Landing your next role isn't about luck — it's about organization, focus, and grit. It's about showing up every day, taking one step forward, and trusting the process. Not a sprint. Not a scramble. A steady, confident stride toward what's next.

One application. One follow-up. One conversation at a time.

The Philosophy

Stride keeps everything in one place so you can focus on what actually matters: telling your story, building relationships, and finding the right fit.

  • No more scattered notes
  • No more "wait, did I already apply there?"
  • Just clarity, momentum, and progress you can see
